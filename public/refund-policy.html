Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. CampusLoop is a student project. Replace the contact email and operator details below with your own before accepting real payments.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order yourself while it is still pending (before the vendor accepts it). After a vendor accepts an order, contact the vendor through the order chat; cancellation is at the vendor’s discretion once work has started.

When you get a refund

  • You cancel a paid order while it is pending.
  • The vendor rejects the order.
  • The service was not delivered, or was clearly not as described (contact us within 48 hours).

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. We start refunds automatically when an order is cancelled or rejected; if that fails, an admin processes it manually. Banks usually take 5–7 working days to show the money.

Coupons

If you cancel or the vendor rejects an order, the coupon you used is returned to you where it is still valid.
