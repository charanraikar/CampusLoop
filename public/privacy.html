Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. CampusLoop is a student project. Replace the contact email and operator details below with your own before accepting real payments.

What we collect

Your name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), orders, delivery location, pickup slots, messages between you and vendors, reviews, and PDF files you upload for print orders. Vendors also provide a public profile (about, hours, area and, optionally, a WhatsApp number that is visible to students).

Why we use it

To create your account, process orders and payments, let you and vendors communicate, send order and security emails (such as password resets), prevent abuse, and keep an audit log of important actions.

Who sees it

A vendor sees the details of orders placed with them (your name, location, slot, notes, messages and any uploaded print file). Admins can view orders and messages to resolve disputes. Payment details are handled by Razorpay under their own privacy policy; we receive only a payment reference and status. Emails are sent through our email provider.

Cookies and storage

We use one essential, HttpOnly cookie to keep you signed in. Your browser also stores your language and dark-mode choice locally. The installable app caches the site’s public files for offline use; it does not cache your orders or account data.

Retention and your rights

We keep order records as long as needed to handle refunds and disputes. You can ask us to correct or delete your account by emailing raikarcharan64@example.com; some records may be kept where required by law.

Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions expire, requests are rate-limited, and files are only available to the ordering student, the assigned vendor and admins. No system is perfectly secure; please use a unique password.
